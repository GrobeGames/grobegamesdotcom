Word Meadow

Where words bloom and brains blossom.

Wander through lush, whimsical meadows where every flower hides a word waiting to be discovered. Swipe to connect letters, watch your garden grow, and let the puzzles unwind you one bloom at a time.

Your Mission

Every Word You Find Brings a Meadow Back

Pippy’s meadows have gone quiet — grey soil, bare branches, not a petal in sight. That’s where you come in.

Every word you trace sends a little life back into the ground. Grass returns first, then wildflowers, then whole hillsides in bloom. Solve enough puzzles and a forgotten clearing becomes somewhere worth visiting again.

Pippy will be right there with you, buzzing encouragement and taking entirely too much credit for the flowers.

How To Play

Three Steps to a Full Meadow

Pippy will show you the ropes — it takes about ten seconds to learn.

Swipe the Letters

Drag your finger across the letter petals to spell a word. Lift to lock it in.

Fill the Board

Every correct word blossoms into place. Find them all to complete the level.

Unlock New Meadows

Clear a meadow and the path opens to the next one — each with its own flowers and surprises.

Peek Into the Meadow

Every screen is a little garden. Swipe or scroll through the meadow — this is the game you'll be playing in September.
